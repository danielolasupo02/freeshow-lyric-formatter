FreeShow Lyric Formatter

Paste lyrics from any site. Clutter and section labels come out, run-on text gets split into lines, and each section is divided into balanced slides ready for FreeShow.

1 · Raw paste

The example is the public-domain hymn "Holy, Holy, Holy" with typical lyric-site clutter mixed in. Replace it with your own paste.

2 · Cleaned lines (editable)

One lyric line per row. [Section] rows group lines and never reach the slides. Empty rows, invisible characters and symbol-only rows never count toward a slide's lines. Type --- on its own row to force a slide break. Editing the raw paste again will replace your edits here.
